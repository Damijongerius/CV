MediaBase

A safe, self-hosted home for my family's photos, videos and files

MediaBase is the project I've been stalling the longest. The idea: give my family a secure way to store pictures and videos of our family, plus personal files, on a server I manage myself. It's built with Go and React and runs in Docker on a Raspberry Pi, and the big next step (nostalgia and face tagging) is waiting on storage.

MediaBase is a project I've been stalling for the longest time ever. About a year ago I wanted to give my family a secure way to store pictures of our family, and to store personal files safely, on something I would manage myself.

I built the first version before AI, and I think it looked pretty fun: I took inspiration from a spinning animation with rings that I thought looked cool — and it did.

Then hard drive prices went to Tokyo. I never had the guts to buy the 8 TB drives, and looking back at it now: what a fool I have been. So the next time the opportunity presents itself, I won't hesitate.

Goal

A place where everyone stores their pictures and videos so everyone can look back on them in a fun way — and where my family can get their memories out of Snapchat+, where all their endless snap photos and videos are stored right now.

Features

  • Secure, private storage for family photos, videos and personal files
  • Go backend with a React frontend, PostgreSQL and Docker, running on a Raspberry Pi
  • A first version with an animated look, built before AI
  • Planned: nostalgia features and tagging by faces and people, using my data and AI skills
  • Planned: a way for family members to move their photos and videos out of Snapchat+
  • Planned: also work as a Plex server, with an NVMe drive for fast copies so 4K can be rendered without bottlenecking the hard drives

How it's built

A Go backend serves a React frontend and stores media metadata in PostgreSQL. Everything runs in containers on a home server (a Raspberry Pi for now). For the bigger plan, two 8 TB hard drives hold the media and an NVMe drive caches copies for fast playback.

Roadmap

  • Buy 2x 8 TB hard drives when the time is right
  • Face and people tagging, and nostalgia views
  • Help family members leave Snapchat+ by importing their media
  • Plex-style streaming, with an NVMe cache for smooth 4K

Dev log

Why MediaBase is on hold

2026-11-20 · problem

The honest reason: storage. A year ago hard drive prices went through the roof and I never had the guts to buy the two 8 TB drives this project needs. Face recognition and a real family archive can only happen with that space.

I'm using a hard drive for my NDW data right now, but that's a single 4 TB Xbox Seagate that I turned into a normal drive after selling my Xbox. It's temporary and not something I'd trust family memories to.

So it waits. When the right opportunity shows up, I won't hesitate.
